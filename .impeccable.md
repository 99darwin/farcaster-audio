## Design Context

### Users
Farcaster-native crypto users who want to host and join live audio conversations while browsing their social feed. They're mobile-first, design-aware, and familiar with products like Warpcast, Rainbow, and Zora. They expect polished, intentional interfaces — not generic startup pages. The landing page targets potential beta testers who should feel excited to try something new.

### Brand Personality
**Playful, warm, inviting.** Juke is approachable and fun — it's a jukebox for conversation. The brand should feel like walking into a great listening bar: welcoming, social, and alive with energy. Copy should be conversational, not corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Bright and inviting hero that transitions to the app's dark navy — bridges the warm landing experience with the immersive dark product
- **Reference:** Arc browser's website — bright, friendly, engaging shapes and animations without being overdone. Rounded forms, playful geometry, generous whitespace
- **Anti-references:** Generic SaaS landing pages, cold/corporate tech sites, overly dark crypto aesthetics
- **Theme:** Gradient blend — light/warm at top, transitioning to dark (#0f0f23) at bottom
- **Animations:** Subtle, purposeful motion. Scroll-triggered reveals, gentle hover states. No flashy particle effects or gratuitous movement

### Color System (from app)
- **Primary accent:** `#D85A30` (burnt orange/terracotta)
- **Secondary accent:** `#855DCD` (vibrant purple)
- **Dark base:** `#0f0f23` (deep navy)
- **Surface:** `#1a1a2e`
- **Text on dark:** `#ffffff`, `#e0e0e0`, `#9898b8`
- **For light sections:** Warm whites/creams derived from the orange accent, with navy text

### Typography
- System font stack (SF Pro on Apple, Inter/system-ui elsewhere)
- Bold weights for headlines, regular for body
- Size range: 14px body to 48px+ hero headlines

### Design Principles
1. **Warm over cold** — Every element should feel inviting. Rounded corners, warm colors, friendly copy. Never sterile or intimidating.
2. **Show, don't tell** — Use visual metaphors (waveforms, audio shapes) to communicate what Juke is before anyone reads a word.
3. **Purposeful motion** — Animations exist to guide attention and create delight, not to show off. If it doesn't serve the user, remove it.
4. **Respect the transition** — The page should feel like a natural bridge from the open web into Juke's dark, immersive audio experience.
5. **Craft in the details** — Micro-interactions, spacing, and typography should feel intentional. Premium without being pretentious.
